In Southern Rajasthan's hard-rock Aravalli landscape, watershed management is crippled by **departmental silos**:


1. **Severe High-Altitude Gully Detachment:** 45.2 tonnes/ha/yr of fertile quartzite topsoil stripped during short, violent monsoon cloudbursts.

4. **Bureaucratic Latency:** Generating a single Detailed Project Report (DPR) for a check dam takes **3 to 6 months of manual surveyor paperwork**.

## Mathematical & Scientific Formulations

Dharohar is backed by peer-reviewed civil engineering and hydrological formulations:

### 1. USDA SCS Curve Number Runoff Depth:
$$S = \frac{25400}{CN} - 254 \quad \text{and} \quad Q = \frac{(P - 0.2S)^2}{P + 0.8S}$$
*Calibrated for Udaipur hard rock metamorphic terrain ($CN = 82$ baseline, reduced to $68$ with terracing).*

### 2. Revised Universal Soil Loss Equation (RUSLE):
$$A = R \times K \times LS \times C \times P$$
- $R$: Rainfall-Runoff Erosivity Factor ($380 \, \text{MJ}\cdot\text{mm}/\text{ha}\cdot\text{h}\cdot\text{yr}$)
- $K$: Soil Erodibility Factor ($0.038$ for shallow lithic leptosols)
- $LS$: Topographic Factor (computed dynamically from Cesium 30m slope gradients up to $36\%$)
- $C$: Crop/Cover Management ($0.38$ degraded scrub, reduced to $0.12$ with silvopasture)
- $P$: Conservation Support Practice ($1.0$ unmanaged, reduced to $0.25$ with contour trenches)

### 3. Silt Delivery Ratio (SDR):
$$SDR = 0.41 \times A_{catchment}^{-0.18} \times \left(1 - \eta_{check\_dam}\right)$$
*Demonstrating an **84% trapping efficiency** for upstream masonry check dams.*
